Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 6, 2026

Zenrop ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or use the Zenrop Courier mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when using our services, including but not limited to:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Delivery addresses (pickup and drop-off locations)
  • Payment information
  • Profile photo

Automatically Collected Information

When you access our platform, we may automatically collect:

  • Device information (model, operating system, unique identifiers)
  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Location data (with your permission, for delivery tracking and courier matching)
  • Usage data (pages visited, features used, timestamps)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain our courier delivery services
  • To process and manage your delivery orders
  • To match you with available couriers
  • To provide real-time package tracking
  • To process payments and send transaction receipts
  • To communicate with you about your deliveries, updates, and promotions
  • To improve our services and user experience
  • To detect and prevent fraud or misuse
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Sharing Your Information

We may share your information with:

  • Couriers: We share relevant delivery details (pickup/drop-off addresses, package details, your name and contact) with couriers assigned to your orders.
  • Payment processors: To process your transactions securely.
  • Service providers: Third-party companies that help us operate our platform (hosting, analytics, notifications).
  • Legal requirements: When required by law, regulation, or legal process.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Location Data

Zenrop requires location access to provide core features such as:

  • Setting pickup and drop-off points on the map
  • Real-time courier tracking
  • Matching you with the nearest available courier

You can disable location services through your device settings, but some features may not function properly without it.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services. We may also retain information as required by applicable laws, to resolve disputes, and to enforce our agreements.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at contact@zenrop.com.

8. Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience. You can configure your browser to refuse cookies, but some parts of the site may not function properly.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child, we will take steps to delete it promptly.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the updated policy on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Your continued use of our services after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:
